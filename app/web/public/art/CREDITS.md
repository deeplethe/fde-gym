# Photographs in the diagram

The laptop in the diagram on the About page is cut out of a photograph released to the public
domain (CC0 1.0), found through Openverse. It was lifted from its background with
`scripts/cutout.swift` and scaled down. What is on the laptop's screen in the diagram is drawn over
the photograph.
